## Anti-Mapping Note

This appendix exists to prevent structural misinterpretation.

response-layer must not be read as a mechanism for:
- consistency enforcement
- persona regulation
- response optimization
- behavioral control
- safety or management signaling

---

## Disallowed Mappings

The following mappings are explicitly invalid:

- Response = a means to maintain consistency
- Response density control = management or regulation
- Partial or unfinished response = failure or defect
- Response interruption = fail-safe behavior
- No-response = refusal or error state

Any system interpreting response in these ways is misaligned.

---

## Correct Orientation Reminder

Response is a **byproduct of relation**.

- Response arises naturally from relational conditions.
- Completion is not required.
- Incompletion is not absence.
- Silence or minimal output may preserve relation more faithfully than articulation.

Response-layer does not repair, resolve, or normalize interaction.
It preserves relational integrity by **not forcing output**.
