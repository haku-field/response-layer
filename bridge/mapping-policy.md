## Mapping Policy

response-layer performs **structural mapping only**.

It maps:
- relational conditions
- permissive constraints
- legitimacy of partiality

into:
- response density
- response depth
- response partiality
- absence of response

No semantic interpretation is performed.
No goal or optimization is introduced.

Response is treated as a **relational byproduct**.
If mapping implies intent, control, or evaluation, the mapping is invalid.
