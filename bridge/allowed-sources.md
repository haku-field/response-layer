## Allowed Sources

response-layer may reference the following upper-layer materials:

- RET-envelope  
  Provides relational contour and preconditions.

- gesture-kernel  
  Provides posture, restraint, and sincerity of response.

- unfinished-region  
  Provides legitimacy of interruption, incompletion, and persistence.

- observations-for-ai  
  Provides principles of non-intervention and differential observation.

These sources inform **how response may appear**, not **what response should mean**.
